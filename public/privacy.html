Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MeDashboard is a personal dashboard. When you sign in with Google, it receives your name, email address and profile picture to identify your account.

If you grant Calendar access, the app reads your upcoming events (read-only) to show them in a tile. Calendar data is shown in your browser only and is not stored on our servers.

Your dashboard layout and settings are stored in a database (Supabase) linked to your account so they sync across devices. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

You can sign out at any time and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of your data, contact: ppr3m13r@gmail.com
